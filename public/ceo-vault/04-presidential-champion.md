# Presidential Champion Strategy

## The Three-Slide Presidential Pitch

**Slide 1: The Problem**
"The Gambia loses D420M annually to digital fragmentation. No national ID. No interoperable payments. No data exchange. Foreign companies own our citizen data."

**Slide 2: The Solution**
"FORTIS OS gives The Gambia sovereign digital infrastructure. One identity. One payment gateway. One data exchange. Owned by Gambians. Run for Gambians."

**Slide 3: The Legacy**
"This is your digital legacy. A Digital Dalasi that works for every citizen. Sovereignty in your lifetime."

## Why a Presidential Champion is Non-Negotiable
1. Civil service will not implement without top-down mandate
2. Donors require government commitment before funding
3. Multi-ministry coordination requires executive authority
4. Media and public trust follows Presidential endorsement
5. Competition from foreign vendors requires executive protection

## Language for the President
- "Digital sovereignty" — not "digital project"
- "National asset" — not "government contract"
- "Your legacy" — not "our product"
- "Gambian data, Gambian ownership" — not "secure platform"
