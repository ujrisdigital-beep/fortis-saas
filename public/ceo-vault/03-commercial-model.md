# Build-Operate-Transfer (BOT) Commercial Model

## Phase Breakdown
| Phase | Years | Activities | Government Role | Operator Role |
|-------|-------|------------|-----------------|----------------|
| Build | Year 1 | Deploy infrastructure, pilot | Approve, facilitate | Build, test, deploy |
| Operate | Years 2–5 | Run national infrastructure | Oversee, regulate | Operate, maintain |
| Transfer | Years 6–7 | Transition to government | Learn, shadow | Train, document |

## Financial Model
| Phase | Government Cost | Operator Revenue | Donor Contribution |
|-------|-----------------|------------------|---------------------|
| Build | D0 | D0 (cost recovery) | D40M (~$850K) |
| Operate | D0 (transaction-funded) | D300M/year | D0 |
| Transfer | D5M | D10M | D0 |

## Revenue Projections (Year 5)
| Revenue Stream | Annual Projection |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Transaction fees (30% of digital payments) | D180M |
| SaaS licenses (government agencies) | D50M |
| Integration & API fees | D30M |
| Development partner contributions | D40M |
| **Total** | **D300M (~$6.5M USD)** |

## Unit Economics
| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Cost per identity issued | D50 |
| Cost per transaction | D0.50 |
| Revenue per transaction (avg) | D1.50 |
| Break-even volume | 10M transactions/year |
| Gross margin (Year 5) | ~45% |

## BOT Risk Allocation
| Risk | Who Bears It |
|------|-------------|
| Technical deployment failure | FORTIS INVICTA (not government) |
| Low adoption | Mitigated via civil servant anchor cohort |
| Revenue shortfall | FORTIS INVICTA during Operate phase |
| Data breach penalties | FORTIS INVICTA (insured) |
| Post-transfer system failure | Government (FORTIS support contract available) |

## Why BOT Works for The Gambia
1. Zero government capital expenditure during build
2. Government pays nothing if system underperforms
3. Full ownership transfer — no perpetual vendor dependency
4. International precedent: Estonia X-Road, India Stack, Rwanda Digital ID
