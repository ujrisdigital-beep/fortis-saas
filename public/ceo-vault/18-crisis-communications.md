# Crisis Communications

## Prepared Statements

### Data Breach
"FORTIS OS takes data protection with the utmost seriousness. We have detected a security incident and have immediately isolated affected systems. We have engaged independent forensic experts, notified the Data Protection Commissioner, and are in the process of directly contacting all affected users. We will provide a complete public report within 72 hours. We are committed to full transparency and will not minimise or delay disclosure. Our response hotline: security@fortisos.gm."

### Political Attack or Smear Campaign
"FORTIS OS is a national asset built for all Gambians regardless of political affiliation. Our contracts are public, our code is auditable, and our funding is transparent. We welcome any investigation. These claims are without foundation and we will respond to any specific, documented allegation. Contact: legal@fortisos.gm."

### Technical Outage
"We are experiencing a service disruption affecting [specific service]. Our engineering team has identified the root cause and is implementing a fix. Estimated restoration: [X hours]. All transactional data is secure. We apologise for the disruption and will publish a full post-incident report within 48 hours."

### Competitor Smear
"We welcome competition. Digital transformation is not a zero-sum game. FORTIS OS focuses on sovereign infrastructure — the rails that enable everyone to build trains. We do not compete with local fintech. We enable them. We will not be distracted from our mission by competitive noise."

### Media Investigation
"FORTIS OS operates with full transparency. All contracts are public at [URL]. All source code is auditable. We welcome investigative journalism — it improves accountability. Please direct specific documented questions to: media@fortisos.gm. We commit to responding within 24 hours."

### Accusation of Corruption
"FORTIS OS has zero tolerance for corruption. All procurement is competitive and publicly documented. All spending is independently audited by [Audit Firm]. We maintain an anti-corruption hotline: integrity@fortisos.gm. We have notified the Anti-Corruption Commission and invite their investigation."

## Parliamentary Briefing Template

**Opening Statement:**
"Thank you for the opportunity to brief the Honourable Committee. FORTIS OS is a sovereign digital infrastructure project that will save The Gambia an estimated D420M annually in digital fragmentation costs and create the foundation for a digital economy serving 2.5 million citizens."

**Key Messages:**
1. FORTIS OS is a national asset — not a private venture
2. Data sovereignty: all data stays in The Gambia, governed by Gambian law
3. Zero cost to government: pilot funded by donors, operations funded by transaction fees
4. Government ownership: BOT transfers full ownership at Year 7
5. Full transparency: contracts public, code auditable, all transactions logged

**Closing Statement:**
"We invite your scrutiny. We welcome your oversight. FORTIS OS exists to serve every Gambian citizen, not to serve any individual interest. We are ready to answer any question, open any document, and submit to any independent review you deem appropriate. Our mission is simple: a digital Gambia that belongs to Gambians."
