## Political Transition Protocol
If administration changes:
1. System is embedded in civil service (not political appointees)
2. Cross-party steering committee provides continuity
3. Legal entrenchment through enabling legislation
4. BOT agreement survives change of government (international arbitration clause)
5. Public awareness campaign frames system as national asset, not government project
6. International donor oversight creates political cost of cancellation

## Data Breach Response Plan (Summary)
1. **0–1 hour:** Isolate affected systems, activate incident team
2. **1–4 hours:** Forensic assessment, determine scope
3. **4–24 hours:** Notify regulator (PURA), notify affected users
4. **24–72 hours:** Full forensic report, public statement
5. **72+ hours:** Independent audit, remediation, regulatory review
6. **30 days:** Post-incident report, system hardening
