## Assessment Methodology
All KPIs are measured using:
1. **System logs** — real-time, automated, tamper-evident
2. **Independent audit** — annual third-party verification
3. **User surveys** — quarterly structured interviews
4. **Financial reconciliation** — monthly bank + gateway reconciliation
5. **Government co-verification** — ministry sign-off on impact claims
