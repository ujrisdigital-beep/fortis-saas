## Donor Pitch — One Pager
**Title:** Digital Dalasi Pilot — Sovereign Infrastructure for The Gambia

**Request:** $850,000 (D40M)

**Use of Funds:**
| Item | Amount | % |
|------|--------|---|
| Technical deployment | $400,000 | 47% |
| Biometric hardware | $150,000 | 18% |
| Training + capacity building | $100,000 | 12% |
| Project management | $100,000 | 12% |
| Legal + compliance | $50,000 | 6% |
| Contingency | $50,000 | 6% |

**Expected Outcomes (90 days):**
- 10,000 civil servants enrolled in digital ID
- Digital salary payments for 5,000 civil servants
- 1,000 digital business licenses issued
- Replicable model for other African nations
- Full audit trail and impact report

**Why This Investment Matters:**
The Gambia is a replication-ready proving ground. Success here enables scale to 15+ West African nations.

## ECOWAS & AfCFTA Opportunity
FORTIS OS is designed for cross-border interoperability:
- ECOWAS digital identity (Gambia leads, others follow)
- AfCFTA trade facilitation (digital trade credentials)
- Diaspora remittance (UK-Gambia digital corridor)
