# Use Cases by Sector

## Government Sector
| Use Case | Current Problem | FORTIS Solution | Impact |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|--------|
| Digital civil registry | Paper, fraud-prone, duplicates | Biometric de-duplication, digital records | Reduced fraud, accurate census |
| Digital driver's license | Forgeable, no national DB | Verifiable digital credential | Reduced forgery, road safety |
| Digital business license | 3–6 weeks, manual | Instant digital issuance | Reduced corruption, faster registration |
| Social payments | 30% leakage to ghost beneficiaries | Biometric-verified disbursement | Reduced leakage, increased trust |
| Tax collection | Cash-based, evaded | Digital receipts, traceable payments | Increased revenue |
| Customs & trade | Manual declaration, slow clearance | Digital trade facilitation | Faster clearance, AfCFTA compliance |

## Financial Sector
| Use Case | Current Problem | FORTIS Solution | Impact |
